MoodDiary is a private mood journal and vent space. Write down what's on your mind, track your emotions, and see your own progress — all while keeping your data 100% on your phone.

Not a medical tool. It cannot replace a doctor's diagnosis or treatment. But it can help you notice patterns in how you feel, day by day.

KEY FEATURES

• Mood journal
Rate today across 4 dimensions (mood, energy, sleep, anxiety). Attach voice notes, influence factors, and optional CBT thought records to untangle your thoughts.

• Vent space
A completely private corner to write what's on your mind. Encrypted and stored only on your device. Voice recording supported.

• Worry closure
Start a worry thread, and record every time it comes back. The day you say "I'm no longer worried", it moves to your memory archive.

• Trends
Cross-mood, cross-vent trend charts — see yourself getting better, one day at a time.

• Self-reflection
Optional self-reflection questionnaires to observe patterns in your feelings. Results are for reference only. Answers stay on your device.

• Crisis resources
Local crisis hotlines (5 regions) are built into Settings — one tap to dial.

PRIVACY PROMISE

• 100% local: all data and recordings stay on your phone, encrypted with SQLCipher
• Zero cloud: no servers, no accounts, no tracking
• Zero ads: no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics
• Exportable: export JSON backups anytime, or delete everything with one tap

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER

This app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. MoodDiary is a personal journaling tool and is not a substitute for professional care. If you are in an emergency, please contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline:

• US: 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline)
• UK: 116 123 (Samaritans)
• International: https://findahelpline.com

For everyone who needs a little support.
