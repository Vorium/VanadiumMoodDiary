App Reviewer Guide — ChronicCare 0.32.0+142

1. No login required. The app is fully local — no accounts, no cloud, no analytics.
2. First-time setup: app shows a 4-step onboarding flow (consent → welcome → medication → done).
3. Daily use: tap the "I took my medication today" button on home → records check-in.
4. Privacy: all data encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). No analytics, ad, or tracking SDKs.
5. Languages: en-US / zh-Hans / zh-Hant (Simplified + Traditional Chinese). Switch in Settings.
6. Crisis resources: accessible from Settings → Crisis Hotlines (5 regions + international). Tap to dial.
7. Voice notes (vent/mood): optional feature, requires microphone permission. All recordings encrypted.
8. The app is fully free in this version. No in-app purchases, no accounts, no web links.
