ChronicCare helps you take medication on time, track your mood, and stay connected with loved ones — all while keeping your data 100% on your phone.

Designed for people living with chronic conditions, mental health challenges, and anyone who needs gentle, daily support to build healthy routines.

KEY FEATURES

• One-tap daily check-in
Did you take your medication today? A single tap records it. Build a streak. Celebrate small wins.

• Medication reminders
Schedule multiple daily reminders. Never miss a dose. Customizable snooze, refill tracking, and "I'm running low" alerts.

• Mood & mental health journal
Daily mood check-ins to track your emotional patterns over weeks and months. Record your thoughts in a private vent space.

• Mental health assessments
Built-in PHQ-9 (depression) and GAD-7 (anxiety) screening. Tracks progress over time with simple charts. Crisis resources available if scores are high.

• Privacy by design
All data stays on your phone. SQLCipher encryption. No accounts, no cloud, no analytics. We can't see your data — neither can anyone else.

• Vent space
A private space to write your thoughts. Encrypted and stored only on your device.

WHO IS THIS FOR?

• People managing chronic mental health conditions (depression, anxiety, bipolar, PTSD, ADHD, and others)
• Caregivers who want to gently check in on loved ones
• Anyone working to build a consistent medication routine

IMPORTANT

ChronicCare is NOT a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is a personal tracking tool only. Always consult your doctor for medical decisions.

PRIVACY COMMITMENT

• 100% on-device: All data, including voice recordings, are stored locally in an encrypted database.
• Zero cloud: We don't have servers, accounts, or analytics.
• Zero ads: No ad SDKs, no tracking pixels, no third-party data sharing.
• Open permission: You can export your data as JSON at any time. You can also delete everything with one tap.
